Living Worlds

Death afflicts infinitely varied universes, and those who arrive in the afterlife similarly hail from infinitely varied settings. Members are encouraged to submit realms for others to source characters from. Keep in mind that not all those from the same world need to come from the same exact timeline—we follow multiverse rules.

Settings should only be submitted if you're writing a characters from that world. For questions about a particular setting, reach out to the member who submitted it.
